Before you ask

Do you ship across India?

Yes. Orders are dispatched from the farm in Bharatpur and delivered across India. Shipping time depends on your pin code; we confirm it on WhatsApp before dispatch.

How do I place an order?

Add what you want to the basket and press Order on WhatsApp. We confirm pack sizes, the final total and your address on chat, then dispatch.

Are the prices on the site final?

Listed prices are starting prices for the smallest pack. Larger packs cost more, and the final total is confirmed with you before payment.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes, by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp a few days ahead and we will arrange a time.
